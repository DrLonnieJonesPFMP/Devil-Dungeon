**Devil's Dungeon**​



**SYNOPSIS**

Devil’s Dungeon is an action-strategy game made with one singular objective: Gameplay. In this game, you create your own original character, choose from the 21 different classes, 126 weapon arts, and 84 spells to create your own, unique battle style. Instead of playing in a predetermined way, you will eventually have to develop your own strategies to succeed. If using items or skills to fully heal your entire party every turn does not sound appealing to you, if spamming the strongest or most "effective" skill over and over again sounds boring or just outright annoying to you, and if skill and strategy matter to you, play this game.





**General​**

Struggle and Learn:

Partake in nerve-racking combat as every single fight in the game is essentially a boss battle. Your opposition will try to outsmart and outplay you, but will they succeed with their well-forged strategies made from the same classes and skills you have access to? Or will you learn to overcome them? Any mistake you make can be your end, as death in this game is permanent.





Adapt and Optimise:

You will have to constantly change and rethink your strategy to stay ahead of your enemies, or perish trying. But remember, you can always make another character, start anew, and fail... until you best them and emerge victorious.





Loot and experiment:

Every fight rewards you with new gear, spells, and arts leading to gameplay experimentation. My goal was and still is to make every single skill in this game viable.





Equipment and Skills:

Primary weapons decide which skill pool you have access to, as each weapon offers unique abilities, of which you can usually equip up to 5. Secondary weapons extend that skill pool. Elemental Rings grant you skills of their respective element from weaker Tier 0 Spells up to Tier 3 Spells, the most advanced ones.





Force your own destiny:

Create and customise your own original character!









**The Battle System​**

Basics:

Both you and your opponent have a "move" gauge. Once it reaches 100%, you can take an action. Most actions consume Stamina (ST) and deal damage, thus reducing the enemy's health points (HP). Once their or your HP drops to 0, the battle ends. Spells and special weapons arts additionally consume mana points (MP) to activate. You can generate MP through either Spells, Rest, or Guard. Rest and Guard are the two basic abilities that restore ST that every character and enemy can use.



Casting:

Certain abilities have cast times when selected which means that they will not execute instantly. Cast times are usually dependent on your Casting or Agility stat. While casting a skill does not require any ST, executing it often does. As long as the execution cost (the required amount of ST) can be paid, the skill will activate.



Cooldowns:

After using a skill, it may go on a cooldown, meaning that it cannot be used until a certain number of turns have passed. Skills with a cast time go on cooldown regardless of execution.



Leave nothing to chance:

Skills never miss, and there is no "dodging", thus no random number generation (RNG) tanks. All abilities either apply a status affliction at 100% or 0%. There is also no luck stat. Besides the damage variation of skills and the 5% crit chance (crits merely increase damage by 25%), RNG is not a thing; instead skill matters!



NO EXCESSIVE HEALING:

You do not out-heal enemy damage and enemies never out-heal yours. There are NO items, but only your skill set and that of your enemy. Both you and your opponent will gradually and constantly lose HP until one of you is defeated. Every single lost point of HP counts and every mistake will be penalized.
